# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person’s private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter’s privacy.
